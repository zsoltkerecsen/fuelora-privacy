Fuelora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

Fuelora is a food, nutrition and exercise diary for iPhone and iPad, published by Zsolt Kerecsen. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Fuelora has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your diary is stored on your device. We do not run a server, and we do not receive, see, share or sell your data. Fuelora does not send your data to anyone.

Information stored on your device

Fuelora stores the following on your device: your profile (sex, birth year, height, weight, goal weight, activity level and goals), the foods, meals and recipes you save, your food, water, exercise and weight logs, your meal plans, grocery lists and fasting sessions. This data stays on your device. Fuelora does not store it in iCloud. If you back up your device, Apple handles that backup under its own terms.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Fuelora reads your workouts and weight to add them to your diary and progress. Fuelora does not write to Apple Health. Health data is stored on your device and is never stored in iCloud by Fuelora, used for advertising or marketing, or sold or shared with third parties. Garmin Connect data reaches Fuelora only through Apple Health, if you have turned that on in Garmin Connect. You can stop syncing in the app, and remove access at any time in the Settings or Health app.

Camera, microphone and speech recognition

Fuelora asks for the camera to scan barcodes, and for the microphone and speech recognition to log food by voice. Fuelora uses on-device speech recognition when your device supports it. Otherwise, Apple processes the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Fuelora does not keep your recordings.

Notifications

If you start a fast, Fuelora can schedule a local notification on your device. These notifications are not sent through any server.

Exporting your data

You can export your diary as a CSV file from More, Data & export. The file is created on your device, and you decide where to send it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries, or delete everything with Delete all my data in More, Data & export. Removing the app also deletes its data. Because we hold none of your data, we have nothing to delete on our side, so you can use your rights to access, correct and erase your data directly in the app.

Children

Fuelora is not directed at children under 13, and the app asks for a birth year of at least 13 years ago.

Health information

Fuelora gives estimates for general wellness and does not provide medical advice. Calorie goals use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, exercise calories use standard MET values, and food values are approximate. Check with your doctor before changing your diet, exercise or fasting, and before making medical decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below.
